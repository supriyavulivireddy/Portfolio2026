What Webless does

Webless embeds AI-powered search directly onto enterprise websites. A user types a question and gets a direct answer pulled from the company's own content using RAG and vector embeddings, delivered in seconds. No redirects. No page hunting. No dead ends.

The product sits in the MarTech and GTM stack. Its buyers are Growth and Demand Gen leaders at B2B companies whose websites carry years of content that users cannot find.

The answer was always there. Webless makes sure the user finds it before they leave.

My Role

I joined Webless as the sole designer when there was no design process. Over six months I ran two parallel workstreams: the end-user search and answer experience embedded on client websites, and the client-facing admin panel that gave enterprise teams visibility and control over the product.

I worked directly with the founder and frontend team, ran usability studies, and made product-level decisions on prioritisation — including which tools to build first and why.

Workstream A — Lightbox

Designing the answer state

Getting a user to ask a question was only half the problem. What happened next had to make them trust the answer. The lightbox had to do four things at once: deliver a concise response, show the depth behind it, avoid feeling empty on first load, and give the user a reason to keep going.

Nine directions explored. Each one testing a different balance between those four. The same answer content sits inside every layout — what changes is the structure around it.

Workstream B — Admin Panel

From informing Webless to owning the output

Webless clients were always part of the setup process — sharing how their content was structured, what questions their users typically asked, what answers mattered most. But that involvement happened in conversations. Through emails. Through calls with the Webless team.

The admin panel brought that same knowledge directly into the product. Clients could now act on it themselves, on their own timeline, without waiting on anyone.

Three tools. One shift: from informing Webless to owning the output.

Tool 01

Analytics Dashboard

Prioritised first. Without visibility into search performance, clients had no way to see value — and the commercial argument collapsed before it could be made.

Workstream C — Component System

Building for speed without breaking consistency

Two workstreams running simultaneously meant design had to move fast without breaking consistency. Every new client was a new implementation — different content, different edge cases, different needs. A shared starter kit built with the frontend team solved this: a full component library covering every variable the product needed.

The result: faster handoff, faster onboarding, more clients shipped.
