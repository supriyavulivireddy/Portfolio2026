Let's talk ☕

Supriya Vulivireddy

/soup-ri-yeah-wool-ivi-red-di/

Hey, I'm Supriya. I'm a product designer who genuinely loves the work. Six years in, across AI, fintech, SaaS, Web3, fraud detection, smart home automation, transportation and mobility, and more, from tiny teams to products used by millions, and I still get the same pull when a hard problem starts to make sense.

I believe that anything, when designed with real care, changes how people experience the world. Not just software. Anything. That's what got me into this and it's what keeps me here.

Human behaviour and mental models are at the centre of how I approach everything. I find that when you start from how people actually think and what they expect rather than what the system needs to do, the right solution tends to surface faster and stick longer. The rest of my approach follows from that: slow the room down enough to ask the questions nobody thought to ask, protect the time it takes to work on the right things, and move fast once the direction is clear. I've spent enough years at the intersection of product, design, business, and tech that I think of those boundaries less as lanes and more as lenses. The best work I've been part of came from using all of them at once. That's the kind of designer I want to keep being.

Professional Experience

Feb 2025 – Aug 2025

Senior UX Consultant at Webless webless.ai ↗

Webless was trying to sell enterprise clients on AI-powered search and then actually make it work for their users. I owned the full design surface: the search experience on the front end, the admin panel clients used to configure it, and the modular system that let us onboard new clients without starting from scratch each time. In six months the client base grew from 3 to 14. Search interaction rates went from 1% to 3.2%. Average session time more than doubled.

May 2024 – Feb 2025

Lead Product Designer at Cashflo cashflo.io ↗

Cashflo needed to fix how enterprises manage accounts payable, a problem with real financial consequences and users who had been living with broken workflows for years. I travelled across the country to understand the problem firsthand, then owned design and strategy end to end. Invoice booking time dropped from over two minutes to under 45 seconds. Six enterprise clients onboarded in the first three months. I also built the design system from scratch, 500 plus components, keeping it lean enough for developers to actually use.
